# Cranberry Compote

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces (about 3 cups) fresh or frozen cranberries
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 navel orange, zest and juice
- 1/2 cup water
- pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Combine cranberries, sugar, orange zest, orange juice, water, and salt in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Bring to a boil, stirring occasionally until the sugar dissolves.
3. Lower heat to medium-low and simmer until most of the cranberries burst and the mixture thickens, about 10-12 minutes.
4. Remove from heat and let cool before serving.
