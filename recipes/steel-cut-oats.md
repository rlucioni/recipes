# Steel Cut Oats

Serves 2.

## Ingredients

- 3/4 Zojirushi cups steel cut oats
- pinch salt
- 3 Zojirushi cups water

## Instructions

1. Add oats, salt, and water to Zojirushi rice cooker.
2. Set timer for when you want to eat. Use porridge setting.
3. When the oats are done, stir in milk to taste. Let it sit on keep warm for a bit before serving and topping with cinnamon, nuts, fruit, etc.
