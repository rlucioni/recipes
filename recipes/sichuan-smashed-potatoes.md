# Sichuan Smashed Potatoes

## Ingredients

- baby potatoes, skins on
- 1 teaspoon ground toasted cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon toasted and ground Sichuan peppercorn
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder, or a spoonful of [chili oil](chili-oil.md) sediment
- 1/2 teaspoon coarse salt
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- green onions, chopped
- cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. Cut large potatoes in half or into thick rounds. Leave small ones whole.
2. Cover with salted water in a large pot. Boil until a knife slides in with no resistance, about 12-15 minutes.
3. Drain and let steam-dry 2 minutes. Smash each piece to about 1/2-inch thick. For any you're not eating today, refrigerate or freeze at this stage and fry when you want to eat them.
4. Fry in a hot skillet in a single layer, undisturbed, until deeply browned and crisp, 4-5 minutes per side. Start skin-side down if you halved them.
5. Mix dry spices together.
6. Toss with spice blend, green onions, and cilantro.
