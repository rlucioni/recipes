# Roasted Garlic

## Ingredients

- garlic
- olive oil
- salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Peel garlic. Coat garlic cloves in olive oil and salt. Wrap in aluminum foil.
3. Place on a wire rack on a pan and roast for 1 hour.

Allow to cool before unwrapping and serving.
