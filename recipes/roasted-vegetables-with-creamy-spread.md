# Roasted Vegetables with Creamy Spread

Serves 4 as a side.

## Ingredients

### Creamy Spread (choose one)

#### Whipped Feta
- 8 ounces feta cheese
- 1/4 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 small clove garlic, finely grated

#### Garlic-Herb Labneh
- 1 cup labneh or whole-milk Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 1 tablespoon minced dill or chives
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

#### White Bean Purée
- 1 (15-ounce) can cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 sprig fresh rosemary
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- black pepper to taste

*(Note: [Hummus](hummus.md) or [turnip puree](turnip-puree.md) can also be used as the base.)*

### Vegetables (choose 1 1/2 to 2 pounds total)
- 1 1/2 to 2 pounds seasonal vegetables, trimmed and cut into bite-sized pieces (delicata squash, butternut squash, carrots, parsnips, Brussels sprouts, peeled [roasted beets](roasted-beets.md), or [sauteed mushrooms](sauteed-mushrooms.md))
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- black pepper to taste

### Garnishes and Accents
- 2 tablespoons toasted pepitas, pine nuts, or chopped pistachios
- 2 tablespoons pomegranate pips
- 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, mint, or dill leaves
- 1 to 2 teaspoons pomegranate molasses, sherry vinegar, hot honey, or [chili oil](chili-oil.md)
- flaky sea salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the cut vegetables with olive oil, kosher salt, and black pepper on a baking sheet, spreading them into an even layer. Roast following the method in [roasted vegetables](roasted-vegetable.md) until tender and well caramelized on the edges, 20 to 35 minutes depending on the vegetable chosen. If including mushrooms, cook them according to [sauteed mushrooms](sauteed-mushrooms.md) or roast them on a separate sheet pan so they brown properly.
3. While the vegetables cook, prepare the spread:
   - For whipped feta: combine feta, yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, and garlic in a food processor and blend until completely smooth.
   - For garlic-herb labneh: whisk together labneh, olive oil, lemon zest, minced herbs, and salt in a bowl.
   - For white bean purée: simmer drained beans, garlic cloves, rosemary, and water in a small saucepan over medium heat for 5 minutes. Remove the rosemary sprig, transfer the rest to a blender with olive oil and lemon juice, and blend until completely smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Spread your chosen base across the bottom of a wide serving platter or shallow bowl, swooshing the center with the back of a spoon to create a shallow well.
5. Pile the hot roasted vegetables and sautéed mushrooms directly onto the spread.
6. Scatter toasted nuts or seeds, pomegranate pips, and fresh herbs over the top. Finish with a drizzle of pomegranate molasses, sherry vinegar, hot honey, or [chili oil](chili-oil.md), and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt. Serve warm with crusty bread or warm flatbread for scooping up the spread and vegetables.
