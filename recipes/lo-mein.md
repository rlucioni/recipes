# Lo Mein

Adapted from [Made With Lau's beef lo mein](https://www.madewithlau.com/recipes/beef-lo-mein). Serves 4.

## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 1/2 pound beef (sirloin, skirt steak, flank steak, etc.) or boneless skinless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- 2 tablespoons water
- pinch of baking soda, if using beef

### Sauce

- 1 1/2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1/2 tablespoon sugar

### Everything else

- 1 pound lo mein noodles
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 4 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces, whites and greens separated
- bean sprouts
- optional vegetables (choose some): bell pepper, napa cabbage, snow peas, carrots, mushrooms, cut into strips
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Mix marinade ingredients. If using beef, add a pinch of baking soda and slice against the grain. Toss with the protein. Reserve.
2. Mix sauce until smooth. Reserve.
3. Boil noodles until cooked through, following package instructions or until there is no raw center. Drain. Do not rinse if stir-frying right away.
4. Stir-fry protein in a smoking-hot wok or skillet until about 80% cooked. Reserve.
5. Stir-fry vegetables. Harder vegetables first (carrot, cabbage stems, mushrooms); quicker ones later (pepper, snow peas, cabbage leaves). Reserve.
6. In batches (2 servings per batch): Stir-fry garlic and green onion whites until starting to soften. Add noodles and sauce. Toss until the noodles soak up the sauce. Add reserved protein and vegetables and mix well. 
7. Add bean sprouts and green onion greens. Taste and adjust with light soy sauce. Finish with sesame oil. Repeat with additional batches.
