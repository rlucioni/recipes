# Bang Bang Ji

Adapted from [The Woks of Life's bang bang chicken](https://thewoksoflife.com/bang-bang-chicken-sichuan/).

## Ingredients

- 1 pound bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 3 slices ginger
- 2 scallions
- splash of Shaoxing wine
- 1/2 seedless cucumber, julienned
- 1/2 cup poaching liquid from the chicken
- 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- 4 teaspoons Chinese black vinegar
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 1/2 tablespoons sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon [chili oil](chili-oil.md)
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame seeds
- 1 teaspoon ground Sichuan peppercorn

## Instructions

1. Cover chicken with water in a small pot. Add ginger, whole scallion, and Shaoxing wine. Bring just to a boil, then drop to a bare simmer. Cook until the thickest part of the thigh hits 175-185°F, about 20-25 minutes.
2. Transfer chicken to an ice bath. Keep the poaching liquid.
3. Discard skin and bones. Shred the meat by hand into long, ragged strands. (If using breast, flatten and shred with a rolling pin.)
4. Spread cucumber on a plate and pile the chicken on top.
5. Mix 1/2 cup poaching liquid with soy sauce, black vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, chili oil, sesame seeds, Sichuan peppercorn, and chopped scallion.
6. Pour sauce over the chicken. Toss just before eating.

Save leftover poaching liquid as a sipping broth or broth for other soups.
