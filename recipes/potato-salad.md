# Potato Salad

## Ingredients

- 5 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, halved
- 6 [hard boiled eggs](hard-boiled-eggs.md), sliced
- 4-6 strips thick-cut bacon, diced
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 1 cup dill pickle, chopped
- mayonnaise
- salt
- pepper
- paprika
- cayenne

## Instructions

1. Add potatoes to a pot of generously salted water. Simmer until potatoes are tender, 25-30 minutes. Slice and reserve.
2. Fry bacon in a pan. Reserve bacon and bacon fat.
3. Combine potatoes, eggs, bacon, onion, and pickles in a bowl along with enough mayonnaise for the mixture to just hold together. Add about two teaspoons of bacon fat plus salt, pepper, paprika, and cayenne to taste.
4. Refrigerate for at least an hour.
