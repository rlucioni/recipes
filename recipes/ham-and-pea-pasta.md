# Ham and Pea Pasta

## Ingredients

- 1 pound elbow macaroni
- 1 1/2 cups diced ham
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 cups peas (fresh or frozen)
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
- green onion, chopped

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta in a pot of boiling salted water according to the package directions until al dente. SAVE SOME PASTA COOKING WATER BEFORE DRAINING! Reserve drained pasta.
2. Brown ham in a pot. Add onion and saute until translucent. Add garlic and sizzle briefly.
3. Stir in peas and cream. Bring to a simmer and cook for 3-4 minutes until the sauce has thickened slightly.
4. Add the cooked pasta, Parmesan cheese, and green onion. Toss to coat the pasta thoroughly, adding reserved pasta water a little at a time if it's too thick. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
